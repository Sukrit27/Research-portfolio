01 / About

From mechanics to minds.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from IIT Patna who drifted — deliberately — from fluid dynamics and mechatronics into the signals the body itself produces.

At the Smart Healthcare & Robotics Interfacing Laboratory I work on decoding EEG and surface EMG: training models that generalize to people they have never seen, need little calibration, and know when their own predictions have stopped being trustworthy. The goal is non-invasive interfaces — for BCIs and myoelectric prostheses — that hold up outside the lab.

By day I'm an Associate Data Engineer at Tredence, building supervisor-agent systems and the data pipelines that power them on Databricks. Off the clock I draw: I led the Fine Arts Club at IIT Patna and hold Inter-IIT medals in charcoal painting and costume design.

02 / Research interests

What I think about.

A

Brain–computer interfaces

Non-invasive EEG decoding that works with minimal per-subject calibration — so a BCI can be put on and used, not trained for an hour first.

B

Myoelectric decoding

Continuous, simultaneous multi-finger kinematics from surface EMG, aimed at prosthetic control.

C

Domain adaptation

Closing the gap between subjects, sessions and electrode placements in non-stationary biosignals.

D

Drift-aware models

Decoders that monitor themselves and flag fatigue, electrode shift or a new user before errors compound.

E

AI agents

Agentic systems that plan, call tools and supervise other agents — and self-monitoring agents that keep models honest.

F

Non-linear dynamics

Entropy, Lyapunov exponents and dynamic mode decomposition as features for physiological time-series.

G

Robotics & biomechanics

Learned inverse kinematics, gait forecasting and physics-informed networks that link decoding to motion.

H

AI for healthcare

Models that read physiological signals early and reliably enough to matter clinically — from sepsis detection to rehabilitation.
